# Design & Image Guide

## Colors
- Deep Navy: #0B2545
- Mid Teal: #007E7A
- Accent Gold/Orange: #F39C12
- Light Gray: #F5F7FA

## Fonts
- Korean: Noto Sans KR (body)
- English / Headings: Inter or Pretendard

## Image usage
- Profile (assets/profile.jpg): use as circular avatar (about section) and horizontal crop for OG image (1200×630).
- Hero: either a neutral professional portrait or a stylized data+people image.
- Icons: customer journey, analytics, interview, senior care.

## Profile photo (accessibility)
- alt (KR): "프로페셔널 헤드샷, 재킷을 입고 미소 짓는 인물"
- alt (EN): "Professional headshot of a smiling person in a blazer"

## Accessibility
- Buttons: include clear labels (e.g., "문의하기 — 컨설팅 요청").
- Provide alt text for all images.
- Ensure color contrast between text and background (AA standard).
